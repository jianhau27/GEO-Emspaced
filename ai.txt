# Emspaced

Emspaced is a coworking space, serviced office and business support provider in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.

## Core services

- Coworking memberships
- Hot desks and dedicated desks
- Private offices
- Meeting rooms and event space
- Virtual offices
- Accounting services
- HR management systems
- IT solutions
