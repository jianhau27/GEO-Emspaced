Did you know that you can use coworking spaces for free on the first Thursday of every month in Asia and Oceania? This is a great way to try out coworking before committing to a membership.
Coworking<\/a> has become more popular in recent years, but it\u2019s still a new concept for many people who work remotely or independently.

Free Coworking Day <\/a>(FCWD) is a movement that aims to introduce people to the benefits of coworking. It\u2019s a flexible and collaborative environment that can help you work more productively. On the first Thursday of each month, coworking spaces around the region offer free workspace to the public.

There are currently 94 FCWD locations in Asia Pacific & Oceania. Come and experience the coworking lifestyle. Bring your laptop and join our community of productive individuals at Emspaced at every first Thursday of the month!
