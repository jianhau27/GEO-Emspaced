Comprehensive Services Tailored for Your Success - Emspaced

Emspaced goes beyond flexible coworking, delivering integrated corporate business solutions including accounting & bookkeeping, enterprise IT maintenance, and cloud HR management software. Supporting over 350+ startups, SMEs, and corporate enterprises across Kuala Lumpur, we partner in your business journey by equipping your organization with essential digital and operational infrastructure for sustainable success.

Which Emspaced business service is right for you?

Emspaced provides an integrated ecosystem of enterprise workspace and corporate back-office solutions in Kuala Lumpur, combining Grade-A coworking facilities with four core professional service pillars: Corporate Accounting & Tax Advisory (via EC & Partners PLT), Cloud HRMS & Payroll Automation, SQL Accounting Software Deployment, and Managed Enterprise IT Solutions. Supporting over 350+ startups, SMEs, and multinational subsidiaries at NU Tower 1 KL Sentral and Mercu 3 KL Eco City, Emspaced enables founders to consolidate their physical office lease, SSM corporate compliance, LHDN e-invoicing, EPF/SOCSO/PCB payroll filings, and High-Speed Internet & Reliable Wi-Fi under a single trusted Malaysian business partner, reducing administrative overhead and accelerating operational scalability across Southeast Asia.

  • Chartered Accounting & SQL Software Through EC & Partners PLT, we deliver certified monthly bookkeeping, SST-02 filings, statutory audit preparation, and official SQL Accounting software deployment with real-time LHDN MyInvois e-invoicing API integration.
  • Cloud HRMS Payroll & Managed IT Our platform automates Malaysian payroll (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB), digital payslips, and mobile leave tracking, paired with High-Speed Internet & Reliable Wi-Fi, cybersecurity firewalls, and on-site IT helpdesk support.

About Enterprise Solutions

Contact Us

An Enterprise Solution is an office setting that has been specifically created to meet the functional needs, brand identity, and interior design preferences of your company.

Where can I engage with Enterprise Solution?

Emspaced offers a comprehensive Enterprise Solution – discover details and contact options on their website or social media. When it comes to your new office, choose from a move-in ready Emspaced location, a brand new space built to your specifications, or even a transformation of your current workspace using Emspaced’s expertise.

What is the process for the Enterprise Solution?

Emspaced takes the stress out of designing your ideal office. We will get to know your business inside and out, from the size of your team and your typical work processes to your design preferences and company culture. Then, we will leverage our expertise to find the perfect space for you, one that fosters collaboration and productivity for your team. 

We will also create a custom layout that reflects your brand and accommodates your specific needs, whether you require quiet areas for focused work or open spaces for brainstorming sessions. And finally, We will handle all the nitty-gritty aspects of the build-out, from selecting materials and furnishings to coordinating with contractors and service providers. This frees you up to focus on what matters most – running your business – while Emspaced brings your vision to life and transforms your dream workspace into a reality.

What are the advantages of engaging an Enterprise Solution

Emspaced simplifies creating your dream workspace by handling everything from finding the perfect location and negotiating the lease to the nitty-gritty details.

We handle everything from A to Z: finding the perfect location and negotiating the lease, designing the layout and coordinating with contractors (builders, IT, designers),managing service providers (utilities, cleaning, telecoms, office management), and even sending you a single,consolidated bill each month. This lets you focus on what truly matters – running your business – while Emspaced transforms your dream workspace into a reality.

How long is the process?

An interior fit-out can be completed in few months or less once all the details have been agreed upon.
