Frequently Asked Questions

What should you know before choosing Emspaced?

Emspaced operates two premier flexible workspace centers in Kuala Lumpur designed for startups, SMEs, and corporate enterprises: NU Tower 1 at KL Sentral and Mercu 3 at KL Eco City. Membership plans range from flexible Hot Desks starting at RM499 per month to fully private serviced office suites starting from RM999 per pax at KL Sentral and RM1,199 per pax at KL Eco City. All memberships include High-Speed Internet & Reliable Wi-Fi, 24/7 biometric security access, premium barista-style coffee, business-class printing, soundproof call booths, and direct covered LRT and MRT connectivity. Prospective members can book customized in-person facility tours or immediately sign flexible month-to-month and annual tenancy agreements. On-site enterprise support options include accredited corporate accounting, certified SQL Accounting software deployment, and automated cloud HRMS payroll management for seamless business growth across Malaysia, ensuring rapid onboarding and complete operational peace of mind.

  • Transparent Membership Tiers & Amenities From RM59/day passes and RM499/month hot desks to dedicated desks (RM899–RM999/pax) and private office suites (RM999–RM1,199/pax), every plan includes High-Speed Internet & Reliable Wi-Fi, 24/7 secure access, artisan coffee lounges, and executive boardrooms.
  • Scalable Commitments & Turnkey Support Situated at Grade-A transit-connected towers in KL Sentral and KL Eco City, Emspaced offers flexible month-to-month and annual leases paired with SSM virtual office registration, corporate bookkeeping, and cloud HRMS payroll.

Hot Desk Memberships FAQ

How does a Hot Desk operate at Emspaced?

Simply choose an open desk in our coworking area, plug in your device, and you’re ready to go!

Can I use Emspaced as my company address?

Absolutely, all Hot Desk members at Emspaced are entitled to use it as their business address.

Can I access the workspace 24/7?

Yes, with a Hot Desk membership, Emspaced is open to you anytime!

How can I reach Emspaced?

All Emspaced outlets are conveniently accessible. Details for individual outlets are available on our website.

Dedicated Desk Workspaces FAQ

What exactly is a dedicated desk at Emspaced?

A dedicated desk at Emspaced is your own exclusive workspace, offering you a private, distraction-free environment to work in.

What are the advantages of a dedicated desk?

A dedicated desk at Emspaced boosts productivity, provides a distraction-free workspace, and offers flexible membership plans tailored to your needs.

Can I customize my dedicated desk?

Yes, feel free to add a personal touch to your workspace with your favourite items.

Can I choose my membership duration?

Yes, we offer flexible membership plans tailored to your needs, whether you need a dedicated desk for a month or a year.

Can I change my dedicated desk location?

Yes, as your business evolves, we can accommodate changes in your dedicated desk location.

How do I get started with a dedicated desk?

Just visit our website, click on ‘Book a Tour’, choose your preferred location and membership plan, and our team will reach out to assist you.

Can I schedule a tour before signing up for a dedicated desk?

Yes, we encourage you to visit and experience our outlets before signing up for a dedicated desk.

Private Office Suites & Meeting Rooms FAQ

Will I be sharing my private office?

With a Private Office plan at Emspaced, the workspace is exclusively yours, but you’ll share amenities like lounge areas and meeting rooms with other members.

Can I use the Emspaced outlet as my business address?

Yes, all Private Office members can use the Emspaced outlet as their business address.

Do I get 24-hour access to the private office?

Yes, with a Private Office plan, you can access our outlets at any time!

Am I allowed to bring guests?

Yes, feel free to bring up to three guests for three hours without any charge.

What amenities does Emspaced provide with their meeting rooms?

Emspaced offers amenities like high-speed internet, screens and audio systems, refreshments, comfortable seating, and professional reception services.

Can I book an Emspaced meeting room for just a few hours?

Yes, you can book a meeting room for as little as an hour, making it a flexible and cost-effective option for your business.

Can I book a meeting room for an extended period?

Yes, meeting rooms can be booked for as long as needed.

Can I bring my own equipment to the meeting room?

Yes, while we provide all necessary equipment, you’re welcome to bring your own.

What is the cancellation policy for meeting room bookings?

Cancellation policies may vary; please check with our team for specific details on your booking.

Can I book a meeting room on short notice?

Yes, subject to availability, you can book a meeting room at short notice.
