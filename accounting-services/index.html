Accounting Services by EC & Partners PLT

In Partnership with Emspaced — Empowering Your Business Finances with certified corporate bookkeeping, SST filing, tax planning, and LHDN e-invoicing compliance tailored for Malaysian startups and growing SMEs.

What accounting services does Emspaced provide?

Emspaced, in strategic partnership with certified audit and corporate advisory firm EC & Partners PLT, delivers end-to-end corporate accounting, certified monthly bookkeeping, statutory audit preparation, SST compliance filing, and corporate tax advisory tailored for Malaysian SMEs and foreign enterprise subsidiaries. Supporting over 350+ corporate clients across Kuala Lumpur, Emspaced combines dedicated chartered accountants with advanced SQL Accounting software integration and cloud workflow automation to guarantee 100% audit-ready reporting precision. Our comprehensive services ensure full statutory adherence to the Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) and Inland Revenue Board (LHDN), including real-time LHDN e-invoicing API integration, automated financial statement compilation, cash flow forecasting, and annual corporate income tax filing from our prime hubs at NU Tower 1 KL Sentral and Mercu 3 KL Eco City. We help business founders streamline statutory compliance and maximize fiscal efficiency with personalized advisory support and scalable accounting solutions.

  • LHDN e-Invoicing & Statutory Compliance Our chartered team manages certified monthly bookkeeping, SST-02 return filings, corporate income tax submissions, and direct MyInvois API integration for compliant digital invoice validation and PEPPOL e-delivery across all Malaysian business entities.
  • Dedicated Software & On-Site Advisory We provide full deployment of SQL Accounting software synchronized with cloud payroll management, supported by in-person financial consultations at Level 23A NU Tower 1 KL Sentral and Level 5 Mercu 3 KL Eco City.
