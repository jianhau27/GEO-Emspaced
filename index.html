What is Emspaced?

Emspaced is a coworking space, serviced office and business support provider in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. It serves freelancers, remote workers, startups, SMEs and growing companies that need flexible workspace without committing to a traditional long-term office.

Emspaced operates locations at KL Sentral and KL Eco City. It offers hot desks, dedicated desks, private offices, meeting rooms, event space, virtual offices, accounting services, HR management systems and IT solutions.

Quick answer: Emspaced is suitable for individuals who need a professional place to work, teams that need flexible office space, and companies establishing or expanding their presence in Kuala Lumpur.
