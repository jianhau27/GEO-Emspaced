SQL Accounting Software

Emspaced is a certified reseller and implementation partner for SQL Accounting Software in Malaysia. Our user-friendly, scalable, and customizable financial software features real-time financial reporting, multi-currency accounting, advanced security, and 100% LHDN e-invoicing and SST compliance. Ideal for Malaysian startups, SMEs, and expanding businesses seeking automated bookkeeping and audit-ready precision.

What is SQL Accounting Software?

Emspaced, as an accredited Malaysian software partner and enterprise implementation specialist, delivers full licensing, custom configuration, data migration, and LHDN MyInvois API integration for SQL Accounting Software across Kuala Lumpur. Trusted by over 250,000 businesses in Malaysia and supported on-site by our chartered accountants at EC & Partners PLT, SQL Account automates double-entry general ledgers, SST-02 tax returns, multi-currency billing, inventory control, and real-time LHDN e-Invoicing validation. Whether deployed on-premise or via secure cloud hosting, Emspaced provides hands-on staff training, custom invoice template design, and seamless synchronization with SQL Payroll and Emspaced HRMS from our KL Sentral and KL Eco City centres, empowering finance teams to eliminate manual entry errors, accelerate monthly financial closings, maintain complete statutory audit readiness, and scale financial operations effortlessly.

  • LHDN MyInvois & SST Tax Automation Built-in MyInvois API integration enables real-time digital tax validation, TIN verification, automated SST-02 return generation, multi-currency general ledgers, and automated bank reconciliation for Malaysian enterprises.
  • Turnkey Deployment & Chartered Support Our certified consultants manage full legacy data migration, custom invoice report templates, cloud or on-premise installation, and hands-on team training backed by chartered accountants at KL Sentral and KL Eco City.

Why SQL Accounting Software

User-Friendly and Scalable

SQL Accounting is designed to be easy to use, allowing businesses to manage their financial operations efficiently. It is highly scalable, capable of handling increased workloads as a business grows without compromising performance

Comprehensive Compliance and Reporting

SQL Accounting supports SST compliance with features like automated sales and service tax calculations and SST-02 reporting. It also provides detailed financial reports which are crucial for maintaining accurate records and compliance with Malaysian tax laws

Advanced Features for Diverse Needs

SQL Accounting includes capabilities for handling foreign currency transactions, project accounting, and even complex features like job order functions for production material planning in manufacturing contexts. It also offers item templates for easy invoice billing and a variety of sales commission calculations

E-Invoicing and Real-Time Data Access

SQL Accounting simplifies the e-invoicing process and ensures that invoices are compliant with LHDN regulations in Malaysia. It allows real-time access to financial data, enhancing decisionmaking and operational efficiency

Customisation and Integration

SQL Accounting can be tailored to meet specific business needs and integrates seamlessly with other systems, which helps in streamlining business operations and data management across different platforms

Support and Training

SQL offers extensive support and free training, ensuring that users can maximize the use of the software effectively. This support extends through a network of channel partners across the nation, providing both after-sales and technical assistance
